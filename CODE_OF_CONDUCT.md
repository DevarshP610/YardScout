# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

YardScout contributors are expected to be respectful, constructive, and welcoming. Assume good intent, discuss technical disagreements with evidence, and focus criticism on the work rather than the person.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized attention, deliberate disruption, and publishing private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through the contact method listed in the repository profile. Include links, dates, and enough context to investigate. Reports will be handled confidentially when possible.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close discussions, or restrict participation when behavior violates this standard.
