YardScout field notes · No. 01

A good find starts nearby.

A few simple steps to find a sale, or put yours on the map.

Made for the weekend wanderer
The pocket guide

YardScout is a community map of upcoming garage sales. Browse first; sign in only when you’re ready to add a listing.

01

Take a look around

Pan and zoom the map to explore. Select a pin to read its date and details. Use the search box in the side panel to filter the listing list by words in a title or description.

02

Put your sale on the map

Choose the sale date, add a useful title and a few details, then click the sale’s spot on the map. Sign in and choose “Pin to Map” to publish it for other treasure hunters.

03

Keep it useful

Use the sale’s approximate public location rather than someone’s private details. Listings are for upcoming sales and disappear after their date.

Listings are community-submitted. YardScout does not automatically search Facebook Marketplace or every classifieds site, so a quiet map may simply mean no one has posted a sale nearby yet.
