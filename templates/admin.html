North America regional sweep

Checks 50 U.S. states, Washington, D.C., all Canadian provinces and territories, plus six major Canadian cities. Runs in the background with saved progress. It checks only sources enabled for each place—not every town or third-party site.

For a focused search, choose a US or Canadian city or town. Coverage depends on the sources available for that location.

Place suggestions use OpenStreetMap via Photon. GarageSaleFinder is enabled for US city pages; eBrandon is enabled for Brandon, Manitoba. Optional Ticketmaster lookup requires a free developer key and is not a dedicated sale feed. Facebook, Kijiji, and other sources are not scanned without approved access.
